# Discrete event simulation modelling to evaluate personalised collection volume strategies in blood donors in England

---
## Yaning Wu, July 2026

These files accompany a manuscript in preparation entitled "Impact of stratified collection volumes on donor safety and blood collection in England: a simulation study". Models have been adapted from published work (Kim et al 2023 [doi: 10.1111/trf.17277] and Qi et al 2025 [doi: 10.1111/trf.18165]) and are fit using 18-month follow-up data from the Strategies To Improve Donor ExperienceS (STRIDES) trial's post-donation testing sub-study.

---

## Evaluated strategies
This study evaluated strategies that deferred blood donors according to their estimated blood volume (EBV) and/or assigned donors to one of multiple collection volumes depending on their EBV and/or other factors. Detailed descriptions of evaluated strategies can be found at https://canva.link/swudi64240seq9v.
