# Discrete event simulation modelling to evaluate personalised collection volume strategies in blood donors in England

---
## Yaning Wu, May 2026

These files accompany [tbc]. Models have been adapted from published work (Kim et al 2023 [doi: 10.1111/trf.17277] and Qi et al 2025 [doi: 10.1111/trf.18165]) and are fit using 18-month follow-up data from the Strategies To Improve Donor ExperienceS (STRIDES) trial's post-donation testing sub-study.
